Albums gain **Hide from All Photos** / **Show in All Photos** in their actions menu. A hidden album keeps every photo in its own view, in search, in other sources, in export, and in backup; only the All Photos presentation changes, and a photo leaves it only when every album containing it is hidden. The menu discloses how many photos stay visible through other albums and offers to open them, the sidebar marks hidden albums, the status bar reports how many photos album settings hide, and the policy is library data carried by backup and restore (manifest schema 8, database schema 28).
