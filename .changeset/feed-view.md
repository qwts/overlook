**Feed view.** A fourth view beside Grid, List, and Moodboard: one reading-width column of cards, each showing the photo's title (the file name until one is set), the image, and the description (a compact placeholder until one is set), with the date, place, and camera underneath. The Feed is a layout of the same virtualized engine, so only the visible cards mount, pages arrive through the same cursor, and selection, favorites, the Inspector, the context menu, albums, filters, and Smart Albums all work as they do in the grid. Images paint the cached thumb first and fade the larger preview in (no fade under reduced motion); offloaded and unavailable photos show their usual states. Closing the lightbox returns to the same card and scroll position, arrow keys walk the column, and a title or description saved in the Inspector repaints the visible card at once. **View → Feed** is now enabled.
