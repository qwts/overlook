Review Duplicates (File menu, ⌥⌘D): Overlook compares every photo's own preview on this device with a rotation-aware perceptual fingerprint and groups the photos that look alike — recompressed, resized and rotated copies — with the evidence for each. Matches are suggestions: Move to Trash is the ordinary delete (protected Originals are preserved and counted), an Original never pairs with a non-Original, intentional variants are never candidates, and nothing is merged or shared. The fingerprints are recomputable index rows beside the photo, never part of a backup.
